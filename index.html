Frequently asked questions

What does a real estate transaction coordinator do?

A transaction coordinator manages the administrative side of a real estate deal after a contract is ratified: tracking deadlines, collecting documents and signatures, communicating with lenders, title companies, and attorneys, and keeping the file compliant through closing. You stay the face of the deal; we handle the details behind it.

What areas do you serve?

We serve agents throughout Hampton Roads, Virginia, including Newport News, Hampton, Norfolk, Virginia Beach, Chesapeake, Suffolk, Williamsburg, and Yorktown.

When should I send you my file?

As soon as the contract is ratified. The earlier we start, the sooner every deadline is on the calendar and every party is in the loop. For listings, our pre-listing service can start before the property hits the market.

Do you handle new construction and short sales?

Yes. Complex transactions with extra parties, longer timelines, and more documents are where an organized coordinator adds the most value.

Do you work with teams and brokerages?

Yes. We work with solo agents, teams, brokerages, and investors, and our process stays consistent whether you send one file or many.
