Transaction coordination services for Hampton Roads agents

Contract-to-close coordination for buyer and listing agents, teams, brokerages, and investors across Newport News, Hampton, and greater Hampton Roads, Virginia.

Buyer side: contract to close

For buyer's agents who want every deadline handled from ratification to keys.

  • Contract review and a timeline of every contingency and deadline
  • Earnest money, inspection, appraisal, and financing tracking
  • Coordination with the lender, title company, closing attorney, and listing agent
  • Document collection, signatures, and regular status updates

Seller side: contract to close

For listing agents who want an accepted offer to stay on track through closing.

  • Contract review and deadline timeline
  • Coordination with the buyer's agent, lender, title company, and attorney
  • Inspection and repair-request follow-up
  • Seller communication, document collection, and closing prep

Seller side with pre-listing

Everything in seller-side coordination, plus support before the property hits the market.

  • Listing paperwork and seller disclosures prepared and tracked
  • Listing launch checklist and coordination
  • A seamless handoff into contract-to-close once you're under contract

Dual agency

For agents representing both sides of the same transaction.

  • Both halves of the transaction coordinated in one place
  • Separate, organized communication with buyer and seller
  • Nothing missed on either side

Compliance review

For agents who manage their own files and want a second set of eyes.

  • File reviewed for completeness and missing signatures
  • Clear list of what needs attention
  • Ready for broker approval

Included with every file

  • A single point of contact who knows your file inside and out
  • Proactive deadline reminders for you and all parties
  • Regular status updates, so you never chase your own deal
  • Organized, compliance-ready documentation at closing
  • Experience with complex files, including new construction and short sales
  • Coverage across Hampton Roads, Virginia
