Meet Tahiri Asencio

Founder of ThynkTask LLC and real estate transaction coordinator in Hampton Roads, Virginia.

TA

I handle the transaction so you can do what you do best: negotiate.

Most transaction coordinators will tell you they handle the paperwork so you can go sell. I see it differently. Selling gets the client. Negotiating is what protects the deal, and that's where your time and attention pay off, not buried in deadlines, disclosures, and follow-up calls.

Hello, I'm Tahiri, the transaction coordinator and owner behind ThynkTask LLC. After six years in customer service and sales management, I started a new career path as a real estate transaction coordinator in 2020, and it's one of the best decisions I've made in my career. My strength is creating streamlined processes that bring peace of mind and win-win outcomes for everyone involved.

Today I serve agents, teams, brokerages, and investors across Hampton Roads, Virginia. I run your files from start to close: deadlines tracked, documents chased, inspections and closings coordinated, so every ounce of your energy stays where it drives the deal: at the table. I know how tedious the buying and selling process can be and how hectic life gets, so I take pride in being a valuable resource for my clients and keeping everything in order.

Inspired by our generation and the ones coming up behind us, I built ThynkTask to help real estate professionals step out of their comfort zone and build the business they've always envisioned.

If you're closing steadily and need someone who can own the process without needing to be managed, let's talk.

Outside the office

In my free time, you'll find me outdoors with my husband and our two children, taking care of our garden, at the beach, trying local restaurants, at the movie theater, or planning my next house remodeling project. I also love swimming and going to the gym.

I'm committed to providing you with the highest level of service, and I look forward to meeting you, building a long-term relationship, and supporting you throughout your real estate journey.

Tahiri Asencio, Founder, ThynkTask LLC
